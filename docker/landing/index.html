What Voidwall does

Four operations.
One inline pass.

Inspect

Parse and scan every outgoing payload across JSON, CSV, TSV, and plain text.

Enforce

Apply policy rules per route, destination, and content type. Block or transform.

Sanitize

Redact, hash, mask, or tokenize matched fields inline. Originals never leave.

Audit

Stream structured findings to your logs. Every match is timestamped and traceable.

Your data, your rules

Define your own
PII types in YAML.

Every business has personal data the built-in detectors will not know: internal employee IDs, customer numbers, project codenames, partner references. Add as many user-defined matchers as you need — with regex, context keywords, confidence thresholds, and per-route scope.

Hot-reloaded — no restart, no redeploy.
Composable with built-in detectors.
Versioned alongside the rest of your config.
See the matcher reference →
detectors/acme.yaml
# detectors/acme.yaml
detector: acme_employee_id
language: [en, fr, de]
match:
  regex: "ACME-[A-Z]{2}-\d{6}"
  context: [employee, badge, staff]
  confidence: high
action:
  transform: redact
  placeholder: "<acme.employee>"
scope:
  routes: ["/api/*", "/g/collect"]
